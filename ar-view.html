What's measured, what's estimated

  • Slope: from your LiDAR scan (or the practice / manual slope you picked). The line is the same physics as the simulator: rolling ball, slope pull, Stimp friction.
  • Where the green sits in the camera is estimated from the phone's tilt sensor, your two taps, and the ball-to-hole distance. iPhone Safari has no web AR (no WebXR), so this page can't use LiDAR depth or ARKit plane tracking.
  • Phone height is worked out from the ball-to-hole distance. If it reads far from where you're holding the phone, the distance or the taps are off.
  • Ball and hole are followed by image matching plus the gyro. Big steps, shadows or a person walking through can lose them — tap Re-tap.
  • The line is pinned exactly at your two taps. Expect it to be a few cm off near the ball and hole and up to 10–20 cm off at the widest part of a big breaking putt.
  • The camera field of view is assumed (70°). Wrong values bend the line slightly.

Tips

  • Stand behind the ball so both ball and hole are on screen, then tap the ball first, then the hole.
  • After the line shows, drag a marker to fine-tune it (moves at half speed so your finger doesn't hide it).
  • Keep the phone upright-ish (screen facing you, camera pointing at the ground ahead).
